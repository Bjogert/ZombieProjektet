# Vision: a familiar Swedish world for a PZ reunion

Build a Swedish world as a **Project Zomboid Build 42 Stable map mod**, beginning in Getaboda and expanding toward Agunnaryd, Södra Ljunga and Ljungby. The creator wants to bring a former PZ group from Ljungby/Agunnaryd back together and surprise friends with recognizable places. Shared play and recognition guide where we spend effort.

- **Geography:** start at 1:1, including connecting roads. Preserve source geometry. Reconsider countryside compression only after travel/performance evidence and a new creator decision.
- **Buildings:** approximately ten creator-selected homes across the expanding world deserve extra exterior/interior care. Build other houses with plausible, varied Swedish templates. Exact interiors everywhere are unnecessary; unknown details are explicitly invented. The first Getaboda version still contains roughly 4–5 buildings.
- **Outdoor character:** decks/patios, gardens with flowerbeds, outbuildings and rural surroundings help establish the familiar setting.
- **Gameplay:** reuse PZ's mechanics. Include hunting firearms in selected rural residential/farm loot as fictional design; tune frequency and balance later. Investigate tractors as desired rural content before adopting a compatible asset/mod dependency.
- **Nature:** permitted geodata guides roads, forest, farmland and water; individual vegetation/details are deterministic. Use flat gameplay terrain initially.
- **Period:** modern geography with Swedish 1990s atmosphere, without exhaustive historical reconstruction.
- **Tools:** a small Swedish building library, simple data contracts and stable-ID corrections that survive regeneration. Focus research on missing geometry and personally important buildings.
- **Collaboration:** develop Getaboda and the tools openly with the creator. Agents handle implementation and technical QA; the creator decides important design choices and tests milestones. Preserve later-area surprises by default.
- **Friends:** private multiplayer is an eventual goal with actual host/client validation. The first proof remains a local singleplayer test, followed by a stable shareable version.

A standalone game is an indefinitely deferred idea, not a parallel implementation or an architecture requirement. See [the specification](PROJECT_SPEC.md) and [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) for current commitments and evidence.
