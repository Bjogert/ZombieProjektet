# Vision: believable Swedish survival

Player promise: Common-sense, real-world survival decisions should usually work.

Consistent rules matter more than feature counts:
- Noise attracts nearby threats; glass and doors behave predictably.
- Wet clothing, cold, warmth, shelter and fire interact meaningfully.
- Objects are useful for visible reasons: a hammer breaks glass or boards windows; a bottle carries water.
- Every action has costs in time, sound, fatigue, materials, risk or attention.
- Architecture, geography and loot should feel recognizably Swedish.

Geography: Ljungby, Ryssby, Kanna, Agunnaryd and rural homesteads; preserve identifiable buildings where datasets permit, selectively shorten empty stretches without breaking roads, bridges or local layouts.

Not planned now: feature parity with Project Zomboid, full multiplayer, exact real-world building interiors or a custom engine.
