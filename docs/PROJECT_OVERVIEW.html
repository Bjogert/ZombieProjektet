What are we building?

A recognizable Swedish world in Project Zomboid. Start with about 4–5 source-confirmed Getaboda buildings, nearby roads and nature, with Swedish 1990s styling and generic interiors where details are unavailable. Later, expand toward Ljungby and test multiplayer with friends.

The synthetic test house and furnished cottage run in Build 42.21.0. The cottage now also has a playable wooden deck, outdoor furniture and three flowerbeds in a separate garden test package. Access routes, outdoor floors, object counts and table collision pass runtime checks. Daylight, a light switch, walls, roof and cottage entrance/window interactions have passed their recorded tests. Creator acceptance and exterior approval remain open. Real Getaboda geometry has not yet been verified or imported.

How we work

The creator controls design and important choices. The lead agent builds, tests, fixes and coordinates. World/data, buildings/art and QA are focused specialist roles used when useful and authorized. Conversation is in Swedish; project material is in English.

Getaboda and the worldbuilding tools are developed openly with the creator. Updates can show layouts, houses, gardens and screenshots, and explain how source data becomes a playable map. The lead handles routine work while the creator participates in design and important choices. Later areas retain surprise by default. Full developer evidence remains available, and bugs are always disclosed.

Getaboda data research

The source audit found 2025 aerial imagery catalog coverage at 16 cm pixel size and a municipal building download from Lantmäteriet. The Geotorget account is active; both building and orthophoto applications have been received and are being processed. Access decisions are pending. Raw geodata and imagery will initially be processed locally in Sweden; cloud/AI uploads await clarification of the conditions. OSM has roads but no building objects in the checked 750 m radius. Actual house dimensions and facade colors remain unverified. The documented automation workflow keeps source facts, assumptions and individual corrections separate.

Established direction

  • Modern geography with Swedish 1990s atmosphere.
  • Decks/patios and gardens with flowerbeds as part of residential character, varied between houses.
  • Local 1:1 scale in Getaboda; countryside distance compression later.
  • Source-backed land cover and deterministic details.
  • Generic interiors; detailed private homes reviewed before publication.
  • Free tools by default; GitHub packages first, Steam Workshop later.
  • Development saves may need restarting; valuable existing saves are protected.
