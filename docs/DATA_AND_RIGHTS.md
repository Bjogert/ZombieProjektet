# Geographic data and rights

- OpenStreetMap/Geofabrik: review ODbL attribution, database derivatives and sharing requirements.
- Lantmateriet: check the specific dataset's terms and access conditions.
- Google Maps/Earth: only use as visual reference unless explicitly licensed for extraction; do not scrape imagery.
- Project Zomboid: proprietary. Do not redistribute its original code, art, sounds or game data.
- Workshop tiles/mods: observe dependencies, permissions and redistribution restrictions.
- Keep source/provenance and licenses with all datasets and art.

This checklist is not legal advice.
